Use the Simple Interest

Enter the financial assumptions shown in the form. The result is an estimate for planning and comparison, not financial, tax or lending advice.

How it works

What the result means

Simple interest is based on principal × rate × time, without compounding previous interest.

Use realistic values from your own scenario and compare several assumptions before making a decision.
