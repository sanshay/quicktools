Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 18, 2026. This starter policy should be reviewed and customized for your legal entity, domain, analytics setup and advertising configuration before launch.

Tool inputs

QuickTools is designed so most calculations and text operations run locally in your browser. We do not intentionally transmit tool inputs to our server unless a future feature clearly says otherwise.

Server and usage data

Your hosting provider may process standard technical information such as IP address, browser type, requested pages, timestamps and security logs. If analytics are enabled, additional usage information may be processed according to the analytics provider's settings.

Advertising

If Google AdSense or another advertising provider is enabled, that provider may use cookies or similar technologies to deliver, measure and personalize ads where permitted. Configure the required consent and privacy controls for the regions in which the site is available before enabling advertising.

Cookies and consent

The base QuickTools build does not require an account cookie. Advertising or analytics services may introduce cookies. Where consent is legally required, use an appropriate consent-management solution before loading those services.
